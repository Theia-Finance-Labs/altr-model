NPV Change Overview

Late & sudden transition NPV against the baseline, summed over every company's asset-level valuation, by region of the assets and by scenario pair. Absolute change is shock minus baseline; percentage change is that difference over the absolute baseline, so a region with a negative baseline can show a large percentage on a small base. Every provider ran on the same code, same assets, same defaults; coverage differs by provider (geographies and technologies the IAM reports), so the company counts are not identical. Region rows count the companies with assets in that region (a company can appear in several); scenario totals count unique companies. IMAGE and POLES map part of the fleet to a Global geography — a coverage artefact of those providers, not a real region.

All absolute magnitudes still carry the carbon-in-fuel-price double count for WITCH, IMAGE and REMIND (target fuel prices embed the carbon tax; the model charges carbon again). Comparisons between regions and between scenarios hold; the headline sizes for those three providers do not until the extract is corrected. MESSAGE, AIM/CGE and GEM-E3 fuel prices are clean; AIM/CGE's target carbon price is missing (zero); POLES's carbon column is as delivered.
