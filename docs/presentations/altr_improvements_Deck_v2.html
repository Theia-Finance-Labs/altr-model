02 / 13

THE PROBLEMTRISK values earnings. Markets value cash.

TRISK's net-profit block is revenue minus a per-MWh cost, discounted. It is an earnings construct — not the cash a firm can distribute.

No investment

No CapEx, no replacement, no decommissioning — the cycles that drive power-sector cash flows.

Fragile margin data

Net profit margins from Refinitiv Eikon: gap-filled averages, unreliable at asset level.

Everything scales with output

All costs production-proportional. Fixed O&M on a stranded plant? Invisible.
Say: valuation practice (FCFF/FCFE) is explicit — use free cash flows, not accounting profits or EBITDA alone. Using earnings double-counts or ignores parts of the cash-flow stream. This is the motivation for every change that follows.
03 / 13

WHAT WE BUILTAsset-level free cash flow, bottom-up

Each firm is the sum of its physical plants — location, capacity, age, emission factor. AR6 trajectories hit each asset; cash flows aggregate up.

Revenue = Q × price− Fuel = Q × fuel/η− Fixed O&M = K × fom− Carbon (net of passthrough)= EBITDA− CapEx streams= FCFF → DCF → NPV

Output: valuation shock = ΔNPV between below-2°C shock and current-policies baseline. Q = production volume (MWh), K = capacity (MW).

Say: no corporate tax, no depreciation, no working capital — deliberately excluded; we don't have that accounting data at asset level and say so. The published paper's claim that asset-level data lowers estimated transition cost by ~7% is kept as the paper's claim, not generalized.

CHANGE 2 · RETIREMENT ECONOMICSDecommissioning is a cost, not a payday

Early versions treated retirement as positive scrap income. Real plant retirement is a net cash outflow. Fixing the sign was the single largest direction lever we found.

Carbontech with expected negative shock — before fix52%
After sign fix67%
Say: a stranded coal plant has no salvage market; what it has is demolition and site-remediation liabilities (ARO-style). Charging 50% of CapEx/MW as an outflow is crude but directionally right — technology-specific ratios are queued as future work.
06 / 13

CHANGE 3 · VALUATIONFour upgrades beyond the discount rate

1 · Technology risk spreads

Carbontech +100bps, green −50bps, both scenarios at 7%. Bolton & Kacperczyk carbon premium; Shell 2024 discloses a 150bps O&G-vs-renewables spread.

2 · Stranding-aware terminal value

3 loss-making final years → TV = 0. Profitable-but-declining fossil → 10-year annuity, not perpetuity. Green → standard perpetuity. (Gourdel 2024 abandonment logic.)

3 · Terminal cash flow normalization

Average of last 3 years, not the final year — one CapEx spike can no longer erase a terminal value. (Damodaran / McKinsey practice.)

4 · Split terminal growth

Fossil 0%, green 2%. At r=7%, flat 2% growth means a 20.4× terminal multiple vs 14.6× at 0% — a ~28% overvaluation of declining assets.
Say: the old design put the risk premium on the scenario (8% shock rate). That punished green winners in the shock too — one cause of wrong-direction results. Moving the premium onto the technology is what the carbon-premium literature actually measures.
07 / 13

CHANGE 4 · CARBON COSTTwo modes, because IAMs disagree about prices

Carbon cost = production volume × carbon price × emission factor × (1 − passthrough). The question is which emission factor — and that depends on the scenario.

full_ef — default

Every tonne pays. Used when carbon costs are not already inside the scenario's electricity prices.

differential_ef

Only emissions above the marginal generator pay. Used when prices already embed carbon — avoids double-counting.

Auto-detection picks the mode from the scenario's carbon-price coverage. Passthrough default 0 — the firm absorbs the full cost (conservative stress-test stance).

Say: the double-counting risk was raised in review as "we need a decision tree" — this is the decision tree, shipped and guarded by a runtime warning when modes are mismatched. Note: cost scales with volume (MWh), not capacity.
08 / 13

CHANGE 5 · PRICESIAM electricity prices don't clear markets

AR6 prices are LCOE-derived and technology-differentiated — every technology sees its own price. Real markets clear at one price set by the marginal generator.

  • Consequence: perpetually negative NPVs for some technologies in the baseline — before any shock is applied.
  • A model whose baseline says "this plant never made money" cannot measure a transition shock against it.
Say: this is the deepest data problem we have with AR6, and it motivated the MCPR layer on the next slide. It is a property of the scenarios, not of our pipeline.
09 / 13

CHANGE 5 · PRICESMCPR v2 — a clearing-price adjustment layer

carbon_explicit

For scenarios with explicit carbon prices: gas-anchored clearing price, technology value factors, paired with full_ef carbon costing.

merit_order_decline

Clearing price declines with renewable penetration (IMF elasticity). Decline applied to the clearing price before value factors — a price floor can't silently negate it.

Status — two-track: internal working configuration, validated on 5 / 5 IAM providers. Externally: an exploratory adjustment pending a methods paper and engineering review.

Say: review consensus was "intuitive but heroic assumptions — explore as a paper." We agree, and we frame it that way externally. Providers validated: COFFEE, GCAM, IMAGE, MESSAGEix-GLOBIOM, WITCH. If AR7/NGFS publish usable clearing prices (expected H2 2026), MCPR becomes validation, not dependency.
10 / 13

CHANGE 6 · SEQUENCINGRetirement by age — a proxy, stated honestly

Companies retire their oldest assets first in the model. Age proxies carbon intensity and retirement priority. It does not claim economic optimization.

  • Older plants are typically less efficient and dirtier — the intensity proxy holds.
  • But a young inefficient plant can be less economic than an old efficient one — so we don't claim the economics.
  • Stranding still happens where it should: the valuation layer zeroes the terminal value of persistently loss-making assets, whatever their age.
Say: upgrade path is plant-level intensity from Climate Trace or AR6 efficiency-by-vintage data (future work). Until then, external claims stay at "age as proxy" — this caveat came out of the deep-dive review and we adopt it.

SUMMARYOne sentence per change

  • Cash flows, not accounting profits — because markets price cash.
  • CapEx made explicit; new-build off until double-counting is ruled out.
  • Retirement costs money — and fixing that sign fixed the most directions.
  • Risk premium moved from scenario to technology — where the evidence is.
  • Declining assets stop growing forever: 0% fossil growth, stranding-aware terminal value.
  • Carbon costs auto-detect double-counting; prices get a clearing-price layer, honestly labeled exploratory.
